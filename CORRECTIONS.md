# Corrections log

**Fifteen Weeks** — corrections received and applied.

Every organisation in the sample was sent its coded row, the evidence behind each score, and the quotations attributed to it, four weeks before publication. This file logs every change made to the dataset or the report after that letter went out, whether the correction came from the organisation or from a reader.

Each entry records the date, the organisation, the code or claim affected, what changed, and who raised it. Nothing is removed from this file.
