Philosophy

Localisation has to mean something, or it's just paperwork.

Too much of what gets called localisation is administrative relocation dressed up as empowerment. The reporting burden moves south. The funding rules and the veto stay exactly where they were. I don't call something local ownership unless the power moved with the paperwork, and I don't treat local actors as passive until a donor grants them some.

The Engagement

Transition Exposure Review

Two weeks · Fixed fee · No day rate

You are closing an office, consolidating into hubs, or handing a programme to partners. The reduction is planned. The transfer usually is not.

  • Which functions can transfer within twelve months, and which cannot
  • The single points of failure that sit with you: banking, compliance, certification, the donor relationship
  • Residual liability after handover: employment, safeguarding, registration, assets, records
  • What a funder would need to see before accepting the plan
  • A costed sequence

Every organisation is at a different point in this, so the shape of a review gets worked out in a first conversation, not picked off a list. If a review isn't the right thing, I'll say so on the call and you'll have lost half an hour.
