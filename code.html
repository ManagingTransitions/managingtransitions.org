    Dataset

    Reliability

    A unit is one organisation and anchor programme. An observer is one coder label. Any unit coded by two or more observers enters the calculation. For the drift test, re-score under a modified coder label such as MT-r2 so the same person's second pass counts as a second observer.

    The statistic is ordinal Krippendorff's alpha, computed per item and never averaged across the codebook. The verdict column uses the bootstrapped fifth percentile rather than the point estimate, which is what section 11 of the codebook commits to.

    Decision log

    One entry per ambiguous or edge case. The precedent field is the point of it: a decision that recurs belongs in the codebook anchors at the next version, not in a note.

    How this works

    This is the codebook made executable. The level descriptors on screen are the anchors, so the rubric is in front of you while you score rather than in another tab. The gates in orange are the contestable rules from section 10, and they have to be ticked rather than remembered.

    The full manual, including the academic warrant for each item and the reliability and content validity procedures, is at diagnostic-codebook.html. The public self-assessment version of the same twelve questions is at diagnostic.html. Weights and level values are identical across all three, so a score produced here and a score produced there are the same number.

    Connecting the CSVs

    Connect folder asks for one folder and then reads and writes three files inside it: td_scores.csv, td_evidence.csv and td_decision_log.csv. Files that do not exist yet get created. After that, every saved entry writes to all three immediately, so the CSVs are the record and this page is only a way of editing them. Chrome and Edge support this, including on a Chromebook. Safari and Firefox do not.

    Where the folder route is unavailable, Import CSVs takes the same three files by name and Export CSVs downloads them back. The workflow is identical, with a manual step at each end.

    Nothing is stored in the browser. Close the tab without saving or exporting and the work is gone, which is deliberate: the CSVs are the only copy, so there is never a question about which version is current.

    What gets enforced

    Tier 3, the organisation's own narrative, cannot carry level 3 or 4 on any item except Q12, where self-report is the construct. Trade press is not offered as a tier at all.

    Every scored item needs a tier, a justification long enough to re-derive the level from, and a source. A constraint flag needs the external requirement named.

    Insufficient evidence is a first-class answer and it never becomes level 1. On Q03 in particular, the absence of a documented refusal is evidence about the record and not about the arrangement.

    One thing this cannot do

    It computes the numbers correctly and it cannot tell you whether the level you chose is the right one. That is what the anchors, the gates and the double-coding are for. The reliability tab is the only part of this that can catch a coder drifting, which is why it is worth running at every fifty entries rather than at the end.
